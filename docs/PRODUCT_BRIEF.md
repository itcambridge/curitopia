# Product Brief — Curitopia

## Product idea
Curitopia is a curiosity tool that helps people keep asking questions, move beyond their first thought, and see where their thinking leads.

## Core principle
**Ask more questions. Suspend judgement. Discover what comes next.**

The app should encourage exploration rather than evaluate whether a question is clever, correct or valuable. Question volume and the visible journey are more appropriate feedback than a creativity score.

## Two modes

### Quickfire
A low-friction, stream-of-consciousness experience. Users enter questions rapidly and in any order. They do not need to connect, label, or assess them while typing. When they finish, Curitopia can show a retrospective view of the questions and a lightweight breakdown of themes or notable questions. In the first frontend prototype, this breakdown may be simple and deterministic rather than AI-generated.

### Focus
A deliberate exploration of a central question or theme. The theme remains visible while the user adds satellite questions. Optional prompt types may eventually help the user investigate, reframe, connect or challenge an idea. Prompts must not be compulsory; free entry remains available.

## Audience and use cases
- Someone who wants to unload a stream of thoughts without organising them immediately.
- Someone exploring a subject, planning an idea, or looking for new perspectives.
- Someone who wants to look back at the questions they generated and notice patterns.

## Product outcomes
- Adding a question feels fast and frictionless.
- Users can tell which mode they are in.
- The orbital visualisation makes a growing set of questions feel tangible.
- Question text stays readable.
- Users can finish a session and review what they entered.

## Non-goals for the initial prototype
- A clinical or therapeutic tool, or claims of guaranteed stress relief.
- Social sharing, collaboration, profiles, achievements, leaderboards or subscriptions.
- AI-generated content, semantic analysis, or AI summaries.
- Persistent cloud storage, authentication or multi-device sync.
- A complex graph editor or full project-management system.

## Product language
Prefer: explore, question, wander, investigate, connect, reflect.
Avoid: creativity score, intelligence score, diagnosis, guaranteed stress relief, or claims that the app can exhaust or reset a specific brain system.
